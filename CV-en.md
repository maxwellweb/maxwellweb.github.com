## Key Achievements

### Load Balancing for News Portals — Grupo Vierci

**Problem:** During the pandemic, news portal servers became overloaded with every breaking news publication, causing cascading outages.

**Analysis:** Investigated server architecture and load patterns, identifying that HTTP service threads accumulated uncontrollably under sudden traffic spikes, exhausting server resources.

**Solution:** Implemented load balancing with intelligent traffic distribution and configured timeouts on HTTP service threads to release blocked resources. This distributed load evenly across servers and prevented saturation.

**Result:** Servers stopped crashing even at peak breaking news traffic. 100% availability during the health crisis with zero further outages.
